# Photo-processing dependencies

The server uses unmodified external packages. This notice does not replace
their license terms. Keep their license files when redistributing containers.
Exact resolved packages and integrity hashes are recorded in package-lock.json.

- heic-decode 2.1.0: ISC; https://github.com/catdad-experiments/heic-decode
- libheif-js 1.23.2: LGPL-3.0; wrapper source at
  https://github.com/catdad-experiments/libheif-js/tree/6ca00b818c0ff51cb2a5c75b9ce97d708083335a
- Its compiled decoder and reproducible build/submodule sources are from
  https://github.com/catdad-experiments/libheif-emscripten/tree/v1.23.2
  and https://github.com/catdad-experiments/libheif-emscripten/releases/tag/v1.23.2.
  Fetch the submodules recursively when obtaining corresponding source.
- sharp 0.35.4: Apache-2.0; https://github.com/lovell/sharp/tree/v0.35.4.
  Its separately distributed libvips/native codec packages have their own
  licenses and notices in node_modules/@img.

The public standalone image retains the decoder packages and their LICENSE
files in node_modules; they are not bundled into an inseparable application
binary. An operator can replace those packages and rebuild the application.
Before distributing binary images, ensure corresponding LGPL source/build
materials remain obtainable as required by the applicable licenses. Review
codec patent/licensing obligations for the intended distribution; an open-source
license is not a patent-clearance opinion.

The HEIC/WebP files in tests/fixtures/photos are original synthetic shapes,
generated by scripts/photos/generate-fixtures.py, not third-party photographs.
